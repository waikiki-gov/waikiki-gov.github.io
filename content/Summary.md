**The Sovereign Nation of Waikiki – Official Summary**

The Sovereign Nation of Waikiki is an independent, unitary state established on 10 March 1999 on the territory of the former Republic of Cuba and subsequently expanded through voluntary union with the Federative Republic of Brazil in 2006. Comprising three equal provinces — Waikiki, Amazónia, and Brazília — the country covers 8,840,000 km² and is home to 210 million citizens enjoying full civic rights and a further population of permanent residents and temporary workers.

Waikiki operates as a democratic dictatorship under a constitutional monarchy. The Head of State is His Majesty King Chease Young, crowned in 2005 following a national referendum. Executive power is exercised by the Dictator, appointed for an unlimited term by Parliament; since 2017 this office has been held by His Royal Highness Prince Raimondo Chease. Legislative authority resides in a bicameral Parliament composed of the Congress (elected proportionally every five years) and the Senate (partially appointed by the Dictator and Chancellor). Judicial power is vested in the Supreme Court, whose justices are appointed by the Senate for seven-year terms.

The capital and administrative centre is Nova Aurelia, a modern metropolis built around ancient monumental structures and characterised by its fusion of pyramid complexes, skyscrapers, and artificial island districts. The official language is English (Waikiki-i variant), and the sole legal tender is the Waikiki Dollar (WUD), issued exclusively by the National Bank.

Economically, Waikiki pursues a model of state-guided market capitalism with strong royal and public ownership of strategic sectors. As of 2024 the country records a GDP per capita of approximately 222,000 USD, zero structural unemployment, an inflation rate maintained at 0.17 %, and consistent annual budget surpluses. Energy supply is 100 % renewable or fusion-based, and the nation has been fully cashless since 2018.

Waikiki is a founding member of the American Union (2007), a permanent member of the United Nations Security Council, a nuclear-weapon state, and, since 2025, a full member of NATO. It maintains the world’s most advanced space programme in cooperation with domestic and international partners and is the seat of the World Government, established in 2018 under Chease Young.

The nation prides itself on its blend of ancient heritage and cutting-edge development, its guarantee of universal healthcare, education, free public transport and internet, and its commitment to environmental sustainability and social harmony.
